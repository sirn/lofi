ui: call malloc_trim when the tree picker closes

Opening /tree on a 300 MB session spikes RSS by ~30 MB. Most of that
is the EventIndex vec built on the IO thread, plus the tree-row
skeletons sent over the picker channel. The picker correctly drops
the Arc and entries on close, but the free runs on the main thread
while the buffer was allocated on the IO thread — glibc routes the
freed pages back to the allocating thread's arena, where they sit
indefinitely. Repeat /tree cycles grew RSS by ~25 MB per open before
plateauing at ~89 MB on a long session.

Call malloc_trim(0) after the picker closes (both Escape and confirm
paths). RSS drops back to the post-resume baseline (~36 MB) on the
first close, and stays there across repeat opens. The new helper is
a no-op on non-glibc platforms so the change carries no portability
risk.

Measured on a 293 MB session:
  open /tree   close /tree
  64 MB        35 MB
  80 MB        29 MB
  57 MB        36 MB
